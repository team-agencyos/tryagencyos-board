Retirement gotchas

Check these before removing anything, months from now. All four fail silently.

  • Ads conversions die on unlink. Conversions owned at the manager level stop recording the moment the old manager is removed. Check ownership before touching any Ads link.
  • Plugin licenses lapse. CMS plugin licenses tied to a retiring email stop updating with no error. Re-register during dual-run, not after.
  • Alerts go nowhere. Monitoring, uptime and form notifications pointing at a retired address fail invisibly. Sweep alert destinations per client before retirement.
  • Verification chains break. Search Console ↔ GA4 ↔ Tag Manager links depend on specific owners. Remove old owners last, one at a time, verifying after each.

The emails

Two per client (three where Google Ads is linked), always sent from the existing trusted address the client already knows — never from the new domain. Swap the highlighted placeholders.

day 1 · from the current address · carries the anti-phishing line

SubjectSmall housekeeping on our side — one new team login to approve

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Quick operational note: we're consolidating our internal tooling under one company system, AgencyOS. Nothing changes about who works on your account, what we deliver, or what you pay.

The only thing we need from you: over the next week we'll send a couple of access invitations for our new team address, team@tryagencyos.ai, on the platforms we already manage for you. Approving each one takes under a minute, and our existing access stays in place alongside it — so there's zero risk of disruption.

Heads-up for security: the ONLY address we'll ask you to grant access to is team@tryagencyos.ai — exactly that spelling. If you ever get an access request from any other address claiming to be us, don't approve it; just forward it to me.

I'll follow up with the specific invitations shortly.

Thanks,
[YOUR NAME]
